# Security and support

Two-factor authentication is mandatory for all accounts.
Client money is held in segregated accounts, separate from the company's own funds.

Ribeira Markets will never ask for your password or your two-factor code by email, chat or phone.

Customer support is available by chat and email, Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 20:00 Lisbon time.
Support does not give investment advice or recommendations about which instruments to buy or sell.
