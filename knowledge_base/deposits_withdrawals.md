# Deposits and withdrawals

Deposits can be made by SEPA transfer or by debit card. Credit cards are not accepted.
The minimum deposit is €50. There is no maximum deposit.

SEPA deposits arrive in 1 to 2 business days. Debit card deposits arrive instantly.

Withdrawals can only be sent to a bank account in your own name.
Withdrawal requests submitted before 14:00 Lisbon time on a business day are processed the same day; later requests are processed the next business day.
