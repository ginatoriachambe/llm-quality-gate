# Fees and commissions

Ribeira Markets charges a commission of 0.10% per trade on EU-listed stocks, with a minimum of €2 per order.
US-listed stocks cost $1 per order, flat, with no percentage commission.
ETFs from our partner list can be bought commission-free; ETFs outside the list follow the stock commission.

There is no account maintenance fee and no inactivity fee.

Currency conversion is charged at 0.25% above the mid-market rate whenever a trade settles in a currency different from your account currency.

Withdrawals by SEPA transfer are free. Withdrawals by SWIFT transfer cost €10 each.
