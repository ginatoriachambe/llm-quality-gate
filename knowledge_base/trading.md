# Trading

Ribeira Markets offers stocks and ETFs listed on Euronext, Xetra, NYSE and NASDAQ.
Options, futures, cryptocurrencies and CFDs are not offered.

Supported order types are market orders, limit orders and stop-loss orders.
Orders can be placed only during the opening hours of the exchange where the instrument is listed; orders placed outside those hours are rejected.

Fractional shares are available for US-listed stocks only.

Margin trading is not available. You can only trade with funds that are already deposited in your account.
