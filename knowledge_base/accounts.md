# Opening an account

You must be at least 18 years old and a tax resident of a country in the European Economic Area to open an account.

To open an account you need a valid passport or national ID card and a proof of address issued in the last 3 months, such as a utility bill or bank statement.

Identity verification usually takes 1 business day. Accounts are available in EUR, USD or GBP; the account currency is chosen when the account is opened and cannot be changed afterwards.

Joint accounts are not supported. Corporate accounts are not supported.
